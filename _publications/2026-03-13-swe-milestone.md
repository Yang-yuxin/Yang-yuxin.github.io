SWE-Milestone (formerly EvoClaw) evaluates AI agents on continuous software evolution. Its DeepCommit pipeline reconstructs verifiable development milestones from repository histories, allowing the benchmark to assess how agents maintain system integrity and manage accumulating errors across a sequence of development tasks.
